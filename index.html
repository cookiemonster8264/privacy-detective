Field notes / 01

Ethics is not the same as law

Ethics: What should we do?

Ethics concerns what is responsible, fair, honest, and respectful. For example, a flashlight app requesting your precise location raises an ethical concern because location access seems unrelated to its main purpose.

Values and responsibility

Law: What are we required to do?

Laws are enforceable rules. Whether an action is illegal depends on the specific law, the facts, and whether that law applies. An action can be unethical without necessarily being illegal.

Legal duties and rights

Case study: A navigation app

Imagine a navigation app that needs your location to provide directions. It also wants to keep your location history for advertising. Collecting location data for directions can be useful; using it for another purpose raises questions about transparency, meaningful consent, and necessity.

Think like a developer: explain what data is collected, why it is needed, who receives it, and how a user can control it. Do not assume every questionable practice is automatically illegal.

Know the law: California's CCPA

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives eligible California consumers rights involving personal information. These include rights to know about collection and use, request deletion subject to exceptions, correct inaccurate information, and opt out of certain sales or sharing.

The law applies to qualifying businesses and situations, not every app automatically. The facts and legal requirements matter. This is a California law; other jurisdictions may have different rules.
